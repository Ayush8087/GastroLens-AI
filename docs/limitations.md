# Limitations

- **Provider coverage:** Google Places supplies structured listings when configured. If it is unavailable or returns no listings, Tavily may supply web search leads without structured addresses, ratings, or hours. Tavily is not a complete location database; results depend on local coverage and do not promise worldwide completeness. Both keys are needed for automatic fallback.
- **Search context:** natural-language parsing currently recognizes only explicit meal names, a small set of common dietary terms, and clear `in/near/around` locations. Other preferences remain search context; they are not converted into authoritative constraints.
- **Identity and freshness:** Google Places supplies listing identity/address/business status. Website name matching is a consistency check, not proof of ownership, and provider records can be stale.
- **Menus:** Tavily is used to find candidate menu URLs and official site links are inspected for menu links. Search snippets are not menu evidence. HTML and text-based PDFs are parsed locally. Gemini may OCR a small scanned PDF when no priced text items were found; those lines are explicitly marked unverified and never qualify a hard budget or dietary constraint. Image-only web menus and complex layouts may still be missed.
- **Diet and allergens:** explicit labels from verified official menu pages and literal avoid terms are surfaced conservatively. The service cannot establish cross-contamination, ingredient absence, halal/kosher status, or allergy safety from silence. Unknown remains unknown.
- **Price and currency:** only printed prices are extracted. Currency is taken from an explicit code or unambiguous EUR/GBP symbol; `$` is intentionally ambiguous. Budget checks need matching currency and do not include tax, tip, or service fees unless printed as part of a dish price.
- **AI features:** request interpretation, structured extraction, scanned-PDF OCR, and translation require `GEMINI_API_KEY`. AI-generated output is validated and remains subordinate to source evidence and deterministic checks. OCR transcripts are not independently verified.
- **Availability:** a provider business status does not establish current opening hours, table availability, or reservation availability.
- **Security:** HTTP(S), private/local destinations, redirect targets, response size, and timeouts are checked. DNS validation is separate from connection resolution; production egress filtering or DNS-pinned transport would improve rebinding resistance.
- **Distance:** text locations and radius values use provider search text. Exact radius eligibility is enforceable only when the browser supplies coordinates and the Places response includes candidate coordinates.
- **No persistence:** PostgreSQL dependencies are present from the foundation but not used. Searches are synchronous and are not saved.
